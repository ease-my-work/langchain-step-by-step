# Returns and refunds

## Coffee beans

Coffee is food, so opened bags of beans cannot be returned.
Unopened bags can be returned within 14 days of delivery.

If beans arrive damaged, stale (more than 30 days past the roast date printed on the bag) or are
the wrong product, we replace them or refund them in full, opened or not. Please share a photo
of the bag and the roast date.

## Equipment

Grinders, brewers and espresso machines can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are
unused and in their original box. Used equipment can only be returned under the warranty.

A ₹200 return pickup fee is deducted from the refund unless the item was faulty or we sent the
wrong product.

## How to start a return

Contact support with the order number. Support checks the order and books a free courier pickup
within 2 working days. Pack the item in its original box.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the original payment method: card, UPI or net banking.
- Refunds are issued within 2 working days of the returned item reaching us, or straight away for
  damaged, stale or wrong items that do not need to come back.
- The money usually reaches the customer's account 5 to 7 working days after we issue the refund.
- Cancelled orders that were never paid (for example, a failed payment) need no refund.

## Refund approvals

Support agents can issue refunds, but every refund needs a second person to approve it before
the money moves. Refunds above ₹5,000 must be approved by a support manager.

## Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund while they are still "processing".
After an order ships, it cannot be cancelled; follow the return process instead.
