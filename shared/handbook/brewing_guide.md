# Brewing guide

## Storing beans

Keep beans in their bag with the valve, or an airtight jar, away from light and heat.
Do not keep beans in the fridge: moisture and smells get in. Beans taste best between 7 and
30 days after the roast date. Grind just before brewing.

## French press

- Ratio: 1 g of coffee to 15 g of water, for example 40 g of coffee for 600 ml.
- Grind: coarse, like sea salt.
- Water: just off the boil, about 94°C.
- Steep for 4 minutes, stir the crust, then press slowly.

## Pour-over

- Ratio: 1 g of coffee to 16 g of water, for example 15 g of coffee for 240 ml.
- Grind: medium-fine, like table salt.
- Bloom with twice the coffee's weight in water for 30 seconds, then pour in slow circles.
- Total brew time: about 3 minutes. Bitter? Grind coarser. Sour? Grind finer.

## South Indian filter coffee

- Put 3 heaped tablespoons of fine ground Chikmagalur Dark Roast in the upper chamber.
- Press lightly with the plunger, pour in 150 ml of boiling water and close the lid.
- Leave for 15 to 20 minutes to collect the decoction in the lower chamber.
- Mix 1 part decoction with 2 to 3 parts hot milk, add sugar to taste, and froth by pouring
  between a tumbler and a dabara.

## Espresso

- Dose 18 g of fine ground coffee and aim for about 36 g of espresso in 25 to 30 seconds.
- Fast and sour? Grind finer. Slow and bitter? Grind coarser.
- Descale the Home Espresso Machine every 2 months.

## Cold brew

- Ratio: 1 g of coarse ground coffee to 8 g of cold water.
- Steep for 16 to 18 hours in the fridge, then filter and dilute 1:1 with water or milk.
