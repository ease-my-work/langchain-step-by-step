# Warranty

## What is covered

All equipment sold by Bean Street Coffee comes with a free warranty against manufacturing faults:

- Hand grinders and brewers (French press, pour-over kit, South Indian filter): 1 year.
- Electric grinders: 1 year.
- Espresso machines: 2 years.

The warranty starts on the delivery date and needs the order number as proof of purchase.

## What is not covered

- Damage from drops, misuse, or using the product in a way the manual says not to.
- Wear parts: burrs that are dull after normal use, gaskets, paper filters and glass carafes.
- Scale build-up in espresso machines that were never descaled. Descale every 2 months.

## How to claim

Contact support with the order number, a short description of the fault and, if possible, a photo
or video. Support decides within 3 working days whether the claim is covered.

If it is covered, we collect the item for free and either repair it or replace it within
10 working days. If we cannot repair or replace it, we give a full refund of the item's price.

## Grinder burrs

Steel burrs on the hand grinder and the electric grinder should last for about 500 kg of coffee.
Replacement burr sets are not sold yet; contact support if your burrs are damaged.
