# Shipping

Bean Street Coffee ships from its roastery in Bengaluru to every PIN code in India.
We do not ship outside India yet.

## Delivery times

- Metro cities (Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Pune): 2 to 4 working days.
- All other PIN codes: 4 to 7 working days.
- Orders placed before 2 pm on a working day leave the roastery the same day.
  Orders placed later, or on Sundays and public holidays, leave on the next working day.

Coffee beans are roasted to order, so bean orders can take one extra day to dispatch.

## Shipping charges

- Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more. Below ₹999 it costs ₹79.
- Express shipping (1 to 2 working days, metro cities only) costs ₹149 on any order.
- Gold loyalty members get free express shipping on every order. See the loyalty programme page.

## Tracking an order

When an order ships we email a tracking number and the courier's name (BlueDart or Delhivery).
Customers can also ask support for the status of any order using the order number, which starts
with "BS-", for example BS-1002.

## Delayed or lost parcels

If a parcel has not arrived 3 working days after its expected delivery date, support opens a
trace with the courier. If the courier cannot find it within 5 working days, we send a replacement
at no cost, or give a full refund if the customer prefers.

## Changing the address

The delivery address can be changed while an order is still "processing".
Once the order has shipped, the address cannot be changed; the customer can refuse delivery
and we refund the order when it comes back to us.
