# Loyalty programme: Bean Points

## Earning points

Customers earn 1 Bean Point for every ₹100 they spend, on every order.
Points are added when an order is delivered and removed again if it is returned.

## Tiers

Tiers are based on what a customer spent in the last 12 months:

- Bronze: everyone starts here.
- Silver: ₹10,000 or more. Earns 1.5 points per ₹100 and gets early access to new single-origin beans.
- Gold: ₹25,000 or more. Earns 2 points per ₹100, gets free express shipping on every order and a
  free 250 g bag of beans on their birthday.

## Spending points

- 100 Bean Points = ₹100 off an order.
- Points can pay for up to half of an order's value.
- Points cannot be exchanged for cash and are not refunded in cash when an order is returned.

## Expiry

Points expire 12 months after they were earned. Customers see their points and tier on their
account page, and support can tell them too.
